How the modules connect

  1. Detect: SACHET, GDACS, USGS and EONET alerts land in Disaster Watch.
  2. Assess: rainfall, river discharge, waves and Sentinel-1 radar show impact.
  3. Protect assets: Orbital Safety screens the satellites we rely on against debris.
  4. Reach people: NavIC Link packs the alert into a short satellite broadcast for areas with no network.
