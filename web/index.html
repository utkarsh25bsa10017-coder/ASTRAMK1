    04

    What is real, what is illustrative

    So judges and users know exactly how far to trust each number.

    Real, live data

    Alerts (NDMA SACHET, NCS, GDACS, USGS, NASA), river discharge (GloFAS), rain forecasts, terrain (Copernicus DEM), satellite orbits (CelesTrak), close approaches (SOCRATES), Sentinel-1 radar.

    Screening estimates

    Public orbit data is accurate to about 1 km, so close approaches are an early-warning screen, not a collision call. Collision chances are illustrative. The safety rating is not an official hazard assessment.

    Illustrative

    The NavIC emergency-message frame is our own demonstration design, not the official NavIC format. Satellite status comes from the public catalogue, not official ISRO status.
